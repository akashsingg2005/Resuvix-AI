How does the AI Resume Builder free tier work?

Every new candidate gets 1 free AI Resume generation and 1 free AI Technical Mock Interview practice session completely free of charge! You also enjoy 100% unlimited free ATS score checks and AI Cover Letter generations. After using your free build, you can purchase a One-Time Service Pass or upgrade to the Unlimited Pro Pass.

What is included in the One-Time Service Pass (₹199)?

The One-Time Service Pass (₹199) gives you 1 additional paid resume build and 1 additional paid AI mock technical interview on top of your free tier allowances, plus complete access to unlock your full 15-section AI ATS audit report with missing keywords and executive rewrites!

What is the difference between One-Time Service Pass (₹199) and Unlimited Pro Pass (₹499/yr)?

The One-Time Service Pass (₹199) gives you full access to tailor 1 additional resume and unlock 1 detailed ATS audit report for a specific job application. The Unlimited Pro Pass (₹499/yr) grants annual unlimited resume generations, unlimited ATS scans, AI Cover Letters, and the AI Technical Interview Coach!

How accurate is the 100-Point AI ATS Resume Audit?

Our ATS scanner evaluates candidate resumes using an exact 100-point allocation system across 8 categories: Structure (15 pts), Contact Info (10 pts), Keywords (25 pts), Skills (10 pts), Experience (15 pts), Education (5 pts), Formatting (10 pts), and Grammar (10 pts). Every score deduction comes with line-by-line feedback.

Can guests check ATS scores without logging in?

Yes! Guests can upload PDF resumes or paste plain text on our free ATS Checker page to view their ATS score gauge and top 2 key preview recommendations without creating an account. When you choose to log in, your scan is saved and automatically restored unlocked!

What payment options are supported for upgrading?

We integrate Razorpay instant payment checkout supporting Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI Instant QR Code, Credit/Debit cards, and Net Banking. Your payment activates instantly upon transaction completion.
