Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2025  •  Your Data, Your Control

1. Introduction

At Resuvix AI ("we", "our", "us"), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered career tools at resuvix.com.

By using Resuvix AI, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information (Provided by You)

Data Type Examples Purpose
Account Details Full name, email address, phone number Account creation, OTP verification, login
Resume Content Work experience, education, skills, projects Resume building, ATS scanning, interview prep
Uploaded Documents PDF/DOCX resumes, job descriptions ATS analysis, cover letter generation
Interview Responses Text/voice answers to mock interview questions AI evaluation and performance scoring

2.2 Payment Information

We use Razorpay as our payment gateway. We do NOT store your credit card number, CVV, or full banking details on our servers. Razorpay handles all payment data under PCI-DSS compliance. We only store:

  • Razorpay Payment ID and Order ID (for transaction records)
  • Plan type purchased (Pro Pass / Single Pass)
  • Transaction timestamp and amount

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Analytics: Pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • IP Address: For security monitoring and abuse prevention

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To generate resumes, analyze ATS compatibility, create cover letters, and conduct mock interviews.
  • AI Processing: Your resume text and interview responses are sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored by the AI model after processing.
  • Account Management: To authenticate your identity, manage your subscription, and track your entitlement usage (free vs. paid features).
  • Communication: To send OTP codes, transaction confirmations, and important service announcements.
  • Platform Improvement: Aggregated, anonymized usage data helps us improve our AI models and user experience.
We Never: Sell your personal data to third parties, use your resume content for advertising, or share your information with recruiters or employers without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Database: Your data is stored on MongoDB Atlas cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Passwords: All passwords are securely hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Authentication: We use JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with HTTP-only secure cookies for session management.
  • File Uploads: Uploaded PDFs/DOCXs are processed in memory and are not permanently stored on our servers after processing.
  • Access Control: Server-side access is restricted to authorized personnel only with role-based permissions.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Resuvix AI uses the following browser storage mechanisms:

  • HTTP-Only Cookies: For secure authentication tokens (JWT). These cannot be accessed by JavaScript.
  • Local Storage: For caching user preferences (theme settings, last used template) and session state.
  • Session Storage: For temporary data transfers between pages (e.g., ATS-to-Interview transfer data).

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon account deletion request, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Payment Records: Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by tax and financial regulations.
  • Interview Sessions: Mock interview history is retained until you manually delete it or request account deletion.

  • Right to Data Portability: Request your resume and profile data in a standard machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time by deleting your account.
